Privacy Policy: Shervin YouTube SEO

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Shervin YouTube SEO is an internal tool operated by Swipe Up Studios to manage the YouTube channels owned by Shervin Shares (Shervin Shares, Shervin Shares Reviews and Tesla in NYC). It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When an authorized channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests:

  • YouTube (youtube.force-ssl): to read video titles, descriptions and statistics, and to update titles, descriptions, chapters and playlists that the owner has approved.
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets): to read the owner's SEO planning spreadsheet and write back the result of each update.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to perform the updates the channel owner approves and to track their results.
  • Sign-in tokens and backups of original video titles and descriptions are stored only on a computer controlled by Swipe Up Studios. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, stored tokens and backups are deleted.
